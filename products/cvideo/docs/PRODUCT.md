# CVideo Product Definition

## Product thesis
CVideo is a video-first candidate discovery product for MENA. It helps candidates present themselves through a short video and helps verified recruiters discover, shortlist and contact relevant candidates. It is not a job board and it is not an applicant tracking system.

## Actors
### Candidate
A candidate owns a professional profile whose primary asset is a 30-second video. The video may be uploaded or linked. A CV/resume is optional and secondary. Candidates may maintain skills, up to five preferred job titles and up to two additional profile subfields. Candidates can see their video watch count and can participate in conversations only after a recruiter/company initiates contact.

Candidate navigation is limited to Home, Messages and Profile. Candidates do not browse jobs, apply to jobs, browse other candidates or enter application pipelines.

### Recruiter / Company
A recruiter belongs to a company context. Recruiters search and filter candidate profiles using approved professional fields such as category, subcategory, job/title, experience, certificates and skills. Recruiters can save candidates, organize saved lists and initiate chat.

Recruiter navigation is limited to Search, Saved Lists, Messages and Company Account.

## Company verification
Company onboarding uses a Country field and Commercial Registration Number. US-specific EIN assumptions are prohibited.

## Product exclusions
CVideo does not include job applications, candidate job search, job posting, ATS/talent pipeline features, public social feeds, public candidate showcases, interview scheduling/live rounds, studio-credit quotas, or AI scoring of faces, personality or voice. Unsupported match/ghosting claims are prohibited. User-facing video controls must not offer quality above 720p.

## Localization
Arabic and English are first-class. Arabic is the default language for this pilot. UI must support RTL for Arabic and LTR for English without duplicating business logic.

## Success principle
CVideo succeeds when recruiters can efficiently discover relevant candidates and initiate useful conversations while candidates can present a concise professional identity without being forced into a traditional application workflow.
